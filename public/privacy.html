Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It doesn't use analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising scripts, and it doesn't sell or share any data. Here's the short version of what does happen:

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are sent directly to me via EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, so I can reply to you. That information is used only to respond to your message — it isn't added to a mailing list or shared with anyone else.

Embedded content

Some project entries embed pages from third-party sites (for example, a GitHub Pages project site). Those pages are controlled by their own hosts, not by me, and may be subject to their own privacy practices.

Hosting

Like any website, the hosting provider may log basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) as part of normal server operation. I don't have access to or use that data beyond what the host provides for keeping the site running.
